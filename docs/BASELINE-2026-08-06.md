## Root cause findings (feeds Phase 1 and Phase 3)

**`statement_enriched_count: 0`.** `run_manifest.provider_status.yahoo_fundamentals.status`
is `"degraded"`, and `failed_symbols` lists **roughly 500+ tickers** — effectively the entire
polled universe, not a small edge case. `rejected_at_each_step` confirms the funnel:
`provider_collection: 527 → statement_enrichment: 394 → publication_limit: 354`, i.e. 394
symbols were attempted for statement enrichment and none produced usable statements.

**Consequence — fundamental coverage collapse.** 15+ statement-derived metrics (`ev_to_ebitda`,
`ev_to_ebit`, `ev_to_fcf`, `return_on_invested_capital`, `gross_profits_to_assets`,
`interest_coverage`, `net_debt_to_ebitda`, `altman_z`, `cash_conversion`,
`price_to_tangible_book`, `fcf_growth_3y`, `operating_margin_trend`, `earnings_surprise`,
`net_buyback_yield`, `stock_comp_to_revenue`) are null in 40/40 published rows. Fundamental
coverage: min 0.21 / median 0.29 / max 0.35.

**Consequence — confidence collapse.** Confidence is `0.65 + 0.35·coverage`
(`scorer.py::weighted_coverage`, applied in `scorer.py:583`), so with coverage pinned near
0.29 the confidence multiplier sits at 0.39–0.48 across the board — matching what the user
observed live.

**Consequence — narrow score spread → rank churn.** `run_manifest.score_distribution`:
count 40, min 51.5, max 57.6, mean 54.64. A 6.1-point spread across the entire published list
means ordinary refresh-to-refresh noise (a metric flickering null↔present, a price tick) is
large relative to the signal, so the ranking reorders visibly between refreshes without any
real change in the underlying businesses.

**Also degraded:** `run_manifest.provider_status.sec_form4.status: "unavailable"` —
`SEC_USER_AGENT` unset, so `form4_insider_transactions` scores 0 symbols regardless of the
Yahoo issue.

These three findings — Yahoo fundamentals fetch failing at near-universe scale, the resulting
confidence collapse, and the resulting narrow-spread rank churn — are one causal chain and are
addressed together in Phase 1 (fix the fetch) and Phase 3 (diagnose + fix residual churn).
