## Product and architecture

ValueSignal is an investment research, portfolio analytics, and planning PWA. React 18.3.1 and Vite 8.1.5 render the client. Firebase Authentication and Firestore hold user-specific data. Python jobs publish versioned static JSON. GitHub Actions performs scheduled refreshes. Netlify hosts the app and authenticated utility functions.

There are no broker integrations and no order execution. Research scores, guidance, risk estimates, and projections are decision-support outputs, not individualized advice or forecasts.

The Home route is eagerly bundled. Other routes and the stock detail sheet load on demand. The production build uses explicit vendor, Firebase Auth, Firestore, and remaining Firebase chunks so the application entry is below the configured 500 kB advisory ceiling.

## Research scoring

The production champion remains `bands` normalization. The cross-sectional model ships beside it as a challenger. The published blend is 78% fundamentals, 18% market behavior, and 4% news.

### Champion and challenger

- Champion: configured absolute scoring bands.
- Challenger: winsorization at the 1st and 99th percentiles, then percentile ranking.
- Sector scope: GICS sector when at least 8 valid names are present, otherwise the full universe.
- Lower-is-better metrics reverse percentile direction.
- Range metrics percentile-rank distance from their configured ideal.
- Nonpositive valuation multiples are not applicable and never floored into a passing score.
- Valuation metrics also publish a trailing 5-year own-history percentile when enough prospective observations exist. It is explanatory only and is not blended into either score.

Every scored row carries champion and challenger values, per-metric normalized scores, confidence, category scores, modifiers, model version, config hash, and universe membership in the point-in-time store.

### Attribution and explanation

Each score starts at 50. Category and component evidence moves it to raw evidence, confidence shrinkage pulls incomplete evidence toward neutral, and bounded modifiers produce the final score. A pipeline invariant rejects any row whose decomposition differs from the published score by more than 0.01 points.

The detail sheet presents four concepts first: Research Score, Confidence, Guidance, and Theme Exposure. Below that fold it shows six factor bars, the waterfall, metric-level peer and own-history context, anomalies, and score history. History remains in an honest accumulating state until 6 distinct stored months exist.

The score dial encodes uncertainty visually. Score controls arc length. Evidence confidence controls opacity and dash density. The number does not change when certainty changes.

## Portfolio concentration and risk

Portfolio weights drive Herfindahl-Hirschman concentration:

`HHI = sum(weight_i squared)`

`effective holdings = 1 / HHI`

Correlation analytics use up to 252 trailing daily returns and require at least 60 common observations. Effective bets are the reciprocal concentration of eigenvalues from the weighted correlation matrix. The diversification ratio is weighted standalone volatility divided by portfolio volatility. Diversification penalties are calibrated from HHI breadth rather than arbitrary linear penalties.

ETF holdings use published sector weights before sector concentration is calculated. Published top holdings are also decomposed, which exposes overlap between direct holdings and ETF constituents. Missing look-through is shown as unresolved exposure with its dollar value.

The covariance matrix also produces marginal and percent contribution to portfolio risk. Percent contributions reconcile to 100%. Historical expected shortfall uses the worst 5% of daily portfolio returns. Tracking error is calculated against the selected benchmark. Active share appears only when benchmark constituent weights exist and portfolio look-through coverage reaches 80%.

## Factor and theme exposure

The factor cache refreshes monthly from the Kenneth R. French Data Library and includes market excess return, size, value, profitability, investment, momentum, and the risk-free rate. Portfolio monthly excess returns are regressed with ordinary least squares. Results require 24 observations and include loadings, standard errors, annualized alpha, alpha t-statistic, R-squared, and a plain-language summary.

An alpha t-statistic under 2 in absolute value is labelled statistically meaningless. Theme exposure is aggregated separately by portfolio weight and never enters the research score.

## Planning and projection engine

Planning leads with the probability that a balance survives to the target age under the withdrawal assumption. The gauge verdict bands come directly from config. Annual return target, monthly contribution, retirement age, real annual withdrawal, and allocation aggressiveness resimulate in a Web Worker when released. The interaction budget is 400 ms.

The engine runs 5,000 block-bootstrap paths with 12-month blocks. It publishes the 10th, 25th, 50th, 75th, 90th percentile paths. The fan chart supports touch and pointer scrubbing and distinguishes projected lines with a dotted treatment.

The dotted median is centered on an adjustable annual return target, defaulting to 15%. Supplied brokerage returns set an evidence-based slider range when available. For the supplied Fidelity snapshot, the range is anchored by the year-to-date and trailing one-year returns, while the saved target controls the actual projection center. Allocation aggressiveness changes volatility around the selected target.

When portfolio history has fewer than 36 months, the engine samples the selected benchmark's long history, preserving volatility and return ordering before recentering on the selected target. It does not repeat or synthesize observed months. The chart begins at Now and continues through the configured plan-end age. Retirement can begin from one configured year after the current age rather than being artificially limited to age 50.

Goals reuse the Finances pool structure. Each goal has a name, target amount, and target date, and uses the same probability engine. Retirement remains the default planning goal rather than a separate calculation system.

## News intelligence

News remains 4% of the blend. The aggregation window is 7 days and exponential decay has a 3-day half-life. Full coverage requires 8 deduplicated, confidence-filtered articles.

- regulatory primary: 1.5x
- established press: 1.2x
- aggregator syndicated: 0.65x
- unlisted sources: 1x neutral weight

Ticker matches below 25% confidence are discarded. Titles at or above 82% similarity count as one story for the same ticker. Filings and commentary are weighted and labelled separately in the interface.

## Mobile visual system

The token system defines canvas, shelf, card, and sheet surfaces independently in light and dark themes. Elevation uses hairline borders instead of shadows. Gain and loss are the only saturated semantic colors, and every direction also carries an upward or downward glyph.

Home and Research use floating pill headers, sectioned shelves, value capsules, local as-of labels, and horizontal rails. Account and benchmark selectors use full-width outlined controls that open sheets. Figures use tabular numerals. All targets are at least 44px.

Acceptance captures cover 390px and 430px in light and dark themes for Home, Planning, Research, and a stock detail sheet. The screenshot harness checks viewport width, horizontal overflow, bottom-nav bounds, interactive target sizes, and reduced-motion behavior.

## Authentication and personal data

Firebase email and password authentication gates portfolio, diversification, insights, Finances, Planning, Alerts, and account-specific controls. Firestore stores portfolios, transactions, snapshots, finance pools, goals, preferences, alert rules, and delivery state. Schema changes are additive and readers migrate older advisor, ETF, and finance documents forward.

Netlify functions verify Firebase ID tokens before protected operations. Refresh dispatch is additionally restricted by the configured allowlist. Secrets remain in environment variables and are not written to browser artifacts.

## Refresh, validation, and evidence

Scheduled refreshes fetch and cache data, score the full screen universe, enrich the published subset, append point-in-time records, validate invariants, and publish static JSON. A failed provider call preserves the last successful artifact rather than replacing it with partial data.

The normalization reports compare champion and challenger ranks, rank movers, sector distributions, and size, confidence, and analyst-coverage correlations. The UI report records every mobile acceptance case. The hygiene report records dependency advisories and production chunk sizes.

## Deliberate limitations

- The application does not place trades or connect to brokerage accounts.
- Scores are evidence summaries, not expected-return forecasts.
- Own-history percentiles and historical score charts require prospective point-in-time depth and do not backfill.
- Active share is omitted without benchmark constituent weights.
- Factor regression is hidden below 24 monthly observations.
- ETF exposure remains explicitly unresolved when the published fund record lacks look-through data.
- Bootstrap distributions communicate model uncertainty, not the full range of real-world outcomes.
